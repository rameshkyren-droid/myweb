Guide

Spin The Wheel: free random picker for decisions, names, giveaways, games, and classrooms

Spin The Wheel is a browser-based random picker designed for fast decisions and fun group moments. You can use it as a random name picker, giveaway winner picker, prize wheel, food picker, truth-or-dare wheel, team picker, classroom spinner, chore picker, challenge wheel, or simple yes-or-no decision maker.

How to use the wheel spinner

Type each option on a new line, choose a theme, adjust the style, then press the spin button. The winner appears in the result panel and in the history list. If you want the same wheel later, save it locally or share it with a link.

Weighted entries

You can give some entries a higher chance by adding a weight after the name. For example, Pizza | 3 | 🍕 gives Pizza more weight than an option using | 1. This is useful for prize wheels, lucky draws, game rewards, and probability-based decisions.

Best use cases

  • Giveaways: paste participant names and download a winner card for proof.
  • Classrooms: randomly pick students, questions, activities, or teams.
  • Friends: pick food, movies, dares, games, chores, or challenges.
  • Creators: use fullscreen mode for livestreams, events, and social content.

Privacy and speed

The wheel runs in your browser. Your entries are processed locally, and local save uses your own browser storage. This makes the tool fast, private, and convenient for everyday use.
